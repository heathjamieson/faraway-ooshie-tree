The Faraway Ooshie Tree

Moana leads eleven Ooshies through the Enchanted Wood, up the Faraway Tree branch by branch, and up the ladder through the cloud to find out which land has come to the top of the tree today.

Sailing in from Motunui

The first climbing party

A fan-made homage to Enid Blyton's Faraway Tree stories, with the Enchanted Wood folk swapped for Ooshie-style versions of characters from the Woolworths Ooshies collections. Everything is drawn in code. Not affiliated with Woolworths, Disney, Marvel or the Blyton estate.
